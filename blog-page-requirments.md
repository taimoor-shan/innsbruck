Objective:
Transform the existing WordPress rental property website into a high-converting, SEO-focused platform targeting tourists, business travelers, and long-term visitors to Innsbruck, Austria.

Scope:

1. Blog & Content Hub

* Design and develop a modern blog archive page.
* Create reusable blog post templates optimized for SEO and readability.
* Include featured image, author, publish date, reading time, related posts, and clear CTAs.
* Add category and tag filtering.
* Ensure schema markup compatibility and strong internal linking opportunities.

2. SEO-Driven Content Structure

* Support destination guides, local attractions, seasonal travel tips, business travel content, and accommodation guides.
* Optimize heading hierarchy (H1-H6), metadata placement, image optimization, and content readability.
* Include areas for FAQ sections and rich snippet opportunities.

3. User Engagement

* Add related articles, newsletter signup section, and inquiry/booking CTAs throughout content.
* Design engaging article layouts with pull quotes, image galleries, content blocks, and highlighted recommendations.
* Encourage longer session duration through strategic content navigation.

4. Design Requirements

* Maintain premium hospitality aesthetic consistent with the existing website.
* Prioritize clean typography, generous whitespace, strong visual hierarchy, and mobile-first responsiveness.
* Follow modern travel, hospitality, and real estate website conventions.
* Ensure accessibility, fast loading performance, and excellent Core Web Vitals.

5. Conversion Focus

* Every blog page should guide users toward property inquiries, bookings, or contacting the company.
* Incorporate trust signals, local expertise messaging, and property recommendations where relevant.

Deliverables:

* Blog archive page design.
* Single blog post template.
* Category/tag archive templates.
* Mobile-responsive layouts.
* SEO and conversion optimization implementation.
* Reusable design system/components for future content creation.
